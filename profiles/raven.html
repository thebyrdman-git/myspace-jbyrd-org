Raven's Profile

About Raven:

*lights black candle and adjusts pentagram necklace*

I am Raven. I walk in darkness and find beauty in what others fear. My wardrobe consists exclusively of black - black lace, black velvet, black leather. Silver jewelry adorns my pale skin, and my eyes are lined with kohl as dark as midnight.

I spend my nights reading Poe by candlelight and listening to The Cure. I collect Victorian mourning jewelry and spend weekends exploring old cemeteries. People don't understand me, and that's fine. I don't exist for their approval.

"We are the children of the night. What beautiful music we make." - Raven's LiveJournal, 2003
